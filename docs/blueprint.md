# **App Name**: ECOONDA: Marine Beauty

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Display and filter ECOONDA's range of sustainable, vegan cosmetics, with categories like Hair Care, Body & Skin, and Facial Care, replicating Zara's grid layout.
- Impact Tracker: Track user's environmental impact (plastic saved, CO2 avoided) using Firestore, visualized with graphs and eco badges.
- EcoReturn System: Enable users to scan packaging QR codes (using Expo Camera), register returns, and earn points, managed by Firebase.
- AI-Powered Product Recommendations: Suggest products based on user's purchase history, sustainability preferences, and skin type. Tool assesses user's profile against product attributes.
- Sustainable Ingredient Information: Provide detailed information about the ingredients of each product and their environmental impact to increase transparency.
- Gamified Eco Challenges: Launch seasonal challenges and campaigns related to sustainable consumption. Award badges, points, and special promotions upon challenge completion to improve user retention.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep navy (#011826) to represent the ocean's depth and ECOONDA's commitment to marine life.
- Background color: Sand beige (#E8DCC4), a light, desaturated tone that suggests natural textures and coastal environments.
- Accent color: Turquoise-green (#3CC9B0), evoking clear ocean waters and a vibrant, eco-friendly feel.
- Body and headline font: 'Alegreya', a serif (for headlines) paired with 'PT Sans', a sans-serif (for body), echoing a modern-meets-classic feel suitable for both headers and product details. The app includes longer text such as descriptions for ingredients and environmental impacts.
- Use minimalist icons related to ocean conservation and cosmetic ingredients. Icons should have a hand-drawn style and line-art details.
- Mirror Zara's minimalist, image-focused layout with full-screen product images and elegant spacing to deliver a premium e-commerce experience.
- Ocean-wave inspired micro-animations on buttons and banners to enhance interactivity and add a subtle nod to ECOONDA’s marine theme.
